# Theme artwork included in this build

The theme art below was supplied by the project owner in `images.zip` and installed into `theme_art/`:

- `god_of_war.jpg` — God of War Ragnarök desktop wallpaper.
- `gta_vi.jpg` — GTA VI Jason and Lucia landscape artwork.
- `the_last_of_us.jpg` — The Last of Us Part II wallpaper.
- `stray.jpg` — Stray landscape wallpaper (new theme).
- `call_of_duty.jpg` — Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II Vondel/Nikto wallpaper (new theme).

Other existing themes retain their previous artwork because no replacement images for those themes were included in the supplied archive.
